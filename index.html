The Artist

WHO IS POWERBOI?

Powerboi, born Emmanuel Chigozie, is an emerging Nigerian artiste and songwriter from Enugu State, currently building his sound from Ogun State.

His music blends Afrolove, Afro-heartbreak and Afropreacher elements, drawing from real experiences, imagination and everyday life.

Origin Enugu State
Based Ogun State
Sound Afrolove
Identity Afropreacher
